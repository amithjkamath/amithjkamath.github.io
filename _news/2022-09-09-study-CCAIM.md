**Summer School:** Attended the University of Cambridge's online [CCAIM AI and Machine Learning in Healthcare Summer School](https://www.c2d3.cam.ac.uk/events/ccaim-ai-and-machine-learning-healthcare-summer-school), organized by the Cambridge Centre for AI in Medicine directed by Prof. Mihaela van der Schaar. The five-day program covered topics including data interpretation, clinical trials, graph neural networks, and synthetic data.
